# Agent agreement does not establish evidence independence

Separate agents, providers or contexts do not establish independent source lineage or scientific confirmation. Compare source acquisition and derivation groups, disclose conclusion exposure and retain contradictions and inconclusive findings. Sensor agreement does not establish cause.

# Research

This directory contains the parallel research tracks that feed Forest Cover Lab decisions.

## Tracks

| Subdirectory | Track | Outputs |
|---|---|---|
| `publications/` | Methods and literature review | Inventory CSV, scoring rubric, methods landscape, shortlist |
| `packages/` | Software, platforms, and tooling | Comparison matrix, qualitative notes, platform decision |

## Rules

- Both tracks run in parallel and feed ADRs in `docs/adr/`.
- Decisions are not final until the corresponding ADR is `Accepted`.
- Reviews must be reproducible: every entry has an author and review date.
